# About Northwind Analytics

Northwind Analytics helps mid-sized retailers understand their sales. Our platform collects point-of-sale data from stores, cleans it, and turns it into daily dashboards and demand forecasts.

The company was founded in 2017 in Manchester, United Kingdom. We have about 180 employees across offices in Manchester and Lisbon, and around 40% of the team works remotely.

More than 1,200 retail brands use the platform, including grocery, fashion, and home-goods chains.

## Mission

Our mission is to give every store manager the same quality of data that large chains have, without needing a data team.
