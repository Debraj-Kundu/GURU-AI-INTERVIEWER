# Careers at Northwind Analytics

## How we work

Engineering teams are small, usually four to six people, and each team owns its services end to end, including on-call. We use a hybrid model: people in Manchester or Lisbon come to the office two days a week, and remote employees meet in person once a quarter.

## Benefits

- 28 days of paid holiday plus public holidays
- A yearly learning budget of 1,500 GBP for courses, books, and conferences
- Private health insurance
- Share options for all permanent employees

## Hiring process

Our process has four steps: an AI-assisted screening interview, a technical interview with two engineers, a system design session, and a final conversation with the hiring manager. We aim to give candidates an answer within two weeks of the first interview.
